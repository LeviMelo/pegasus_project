# The overhaul (2026-10-06)

**ARCHITECTURE revision 3** (ADR-0029, 2026-10-06) re-cut this plan to the six stages of ARCHITECTURE §1.1 and changed its order. The current order is **N1 → N2 → O6 → O7 → O8, with O3 and O9 alongside, then O10** (ARCHITECTURE §12). N1 and N2 are new packages, written below; the O-packages keep their sections, each annotated with its stage. The review behind revision 3 is `docs/discussion/2026-10-06-course-correction.md`. **ARCHITECTURE revision 2** (ADR-0023) says *what* PegaSUS is now. The speed design of O1, with its measurements, is `docs/plans/2026-10-06-optimization.md`. This file says *in what order it is built*, what each step must show before the next starts, and what is running. The reasons are in `docs/discussion/2026-10-06-architecture-review.md`.

## Rules for every package

- **One package at a time,** finished before the next starts: built, measured, an evaluation entry, a decision. Results that arrive from earlier runs are read when they land, inside the package they belong to.
- **Literature first (P11).** A package's first step names the established method for its estimand and its reference implementation (R or Python), and states whether PegaSUS's version must match it or beat it.
- **Benchmarked (P13).**
  - A solver change runs the benchmark (`bench`, O1).
  - A detection or relation method runs its slice of the planted grid before it is compared with what it replaces.
- **Machine discipline.** Heavy jobs go through `scripts/heavy.py`, and chains through `data/chain.py`. Few fat jobs, all cores. A job's watcher catches both its completion and its failure.

## N1: the expectation's noise structure (stage B; revision 3, first)

1. **Literature first.** The established forms for serially correlated residual variation in count GLMMs:
   - a place × period random effect with AR(1) correlation over periods (the GMRF of an AR(1) precision, as in INLA's `ar1` and space–time models of the Knorr-Held type);
   - its strength and correlation learned by the marginal likelihood (LAML, §5.4).

   PegaSUS's version must match their predictive calibration.
2. **Measured first** (done 2026-10-06, `data/probes/residual_autocorr.json`): B1 residuals' lag-1 autocorrelation within places is 0.03 on stroke, 0.23 on ill-defined causes, 0.27 on births, 0.11 on congenital syphilis and 0.39 on SIH pneumonia.
3. **Build** the term in the monolith and the solver. The place × period cells are already the place blocks' rows, and an AR(1) precision is tridiagonal per place, so the arrowhead keeps its structure. Then the predictive carries it (the PIT, window sums, the tiers' variances).
4. **Accepted when:**
   - the negatives that failed by serial correlation (SIH trends; SINASC and SINAN trends and space–time) pass with **no per-system constant**;
   - held-out likelihood does not fall;
   - the fit stays within its budget.

   Then ADR-0026's table and the trend relevance floor are removed.

## N2: marginal uncertainty (stage B)

1. **The defect** (evaluation 2026-10-06, SBC): the joint mode puts data-poor place effects at zero and their mass in the intercept, so Laplace draws miscalibrate the intercept and every count-scale sum, on sparse VII and dense XIII alike.
2. **Literature first:** INLA's nested Laplace (marginals of the hyper-level and of the levels), or importance-corrected draws (Pareto-smoothed importance sampling).
3. **Accepted when** SBC's intercept and group totals are uniform on VII and XIII. Departure models report posteriors and need this.

## O1: the solver (stage B; done)

**Steps:**
1. **Assemble the Hessian from the factorised μ**, block by block (leaf-place diagonal, place blocks, the place–global coupling, the global block). Check it against autodiff Hessian–vector products on random vectors for SIM.DO VII, IX and a monthly SINAN block, to relative error 10⁻⁸.
2. **Eliminate in closed form, then factor:**
   - the leaf-place parameters eliminated in closed form;
   - the place system factored by sparse Cholesky (CHOLMOD through scikit-sparse, installed into `pegasus` and declared in `pyproject.toml`; SuperLU fallback);
   - the Schur complement on the globals, dense.
   - Constraints by contrast bases, with kriging for the ICAR components.
3. **Exact Newton** with the existing line search. A preconditioned-CG path on the exact product is used where the global block is large (monthly grain).
4. **BYM2:** one σ and the mixing ρ per graph term.
5. **LAML in log τ** (Newton or BFGS), its gradient by selected inversion. Fellner–Schall's fixed point is kept only as a cross-check of the optimum.
6. **Selected inversion and exact draws** replace `laplace.py`'s perturbation draws.
7. **The benchmark** (`bench`) over the four benchmark blocks of ARCHITECTURE §5.8, cold and warm.

**Accepted when:**
- the v0 optimum is reached on the benchmark blocks (objective within one log-likelihood unit, the τ's within tolerance, the same calibration);
- the budgets of §5.8 are met, or revised with the reason;
- the evaluation entry records v0 against v1 for every block.

**Then:** the v0 solver path is removed, and with it `_trace_inv_times`, `move_tol`'s ridge workaround, and Anderson on the fixed point. No second solver is kept beside the first.

## O5: characterise (the statistical bench for stages B–D)

**Revision 3:** O5 is the bench on which N1, O6 and O7 are measured: the grid, null worlds, negatives and SBC. Documented events move to O8 (stage E). The v0 constants it re-made on 2026-10-06 (ADR-0026) were a stage-C answer to a stage-B defect, and go with N1.

1. **The planted grid of ARCHITECTURE §10.3** over real fitted fields of SIM, SIH, SINASC and SINAN, annual and monthly.
   - *2026-10-06:* a refit absorbs 9–63 % of a planted departure (evaluation, absorption), so worlds are refitted, at fixed strengths, which re-learning does not change. `harness.grid` and `pegasus-core grid` are built: plants by kind × shape × θ, NB worlds from the fit, the production lenses, the power surface, the absorbed share, the false leads, and a θ0 sweep. First field: SIM I60-I69, then a θ0 calibration on I60-I69, I00-I02 and SIH J09-J18 (`data/queue_grid_calib.py`). Not yet: SINASC, SINAN, monthly fields, the seasonal shape, lagged responses (O7).
   - Leads are sized against the fit with their locus held out (`Session.held_out`).
2. **Null worlds** (§10.4) and the existing negatives (§10.2).
3. **Re-make every v0 constant on the grid:** θ0 per lens, the trend's δ per scale, group sd, the alarm's history rule, the trend reference, and the triage thresholds (FAC_K, FAC_SHARE, ×1.6, z ≥ 3). The documented events are then scored, held out.
4. **IHW weights** (§8.4) from the power surface; every result's minimum detectable effect.
   - *2026-10-06:* `control.bh(weights=)` (the weighted BH of Genovese, Roeder & Wasserman 2006) and `control.optimal_weights` (Roeder & Wasserman 2009: each hypothesis weighted by its standardised effect at the reference ratio, from its own μ and dispersion, never its count). Simulated: FDR held, power on a stroke-like spread of cell counts 0.139 → 0.154. In a sparse field nothing helps, because there is no power to redistribute.
   - The grid's pooled surfaces are not used as weights: at equal expected counts SIH detects far less than SIM (dispersion), so a curve by count alone mis-weights.
   - Wiring follows with admission's replacement (step 5), where the weights act across fields.
5. **Retire the gate in code:** done 2026-10-06 (ADR-0028).
   - `tools.SURVEY_PLAN` runs every lens;
   - `gate` became `method`, a lead's record of its method;
   - admission is removed rather than kept as a covariate: the weights read each hypothesis's own expectation and dispersion.

## O6: departure models (stage C; after N1, before O7)

**Revision 3:** O6 is detection proper and comes before relations. The lenses are its screens, and nothing in them is tuned further. Order: cell excess (Efron's two-group model, local fdr), step (Bayesian change point on the past-course reference of ADR-0027), trend (BaySTDetect), cluster (BYM2 exceedance), group interaction. Each is read through its posterior with the Bayesian FDR, its relevance as P(effect > minimum), and is accepted when it matches or beats its lens on the grid at equal FDR.

For each estimand of ARCHITECTURE §7.0, in this order: unusual trend (BaySTDetect), cell excess, step, cluster exceedance, group interaction.
- **Reference implementations first:** `surveillance` (R) for Farrington/Noufaily and `hhh4`; the BaySTDetect paper's model; `locfdr` for the two-group model.
- **Compared with the v0 lens on the grid at equal FDR.** The better one is the inference; the other is kept as the screen or removed.

## O7: relation models (stage D; design note first)

**Revision 3:** relations are **one joint model of all fields' departures**: shared latent space–time factors across fields (the cross-block generalisation of ψωτ; it absorbs O10's top model) and sparse lagged dependence among them. Its cost scales with the number of factors, not the square of the number of fields. The design note is `docs/plans/2026-10-06-o7-joint-relations.md` (for the author's review). `relations.distributed_lag` (built 2026-10-06) is the confirmation tool for a link the joint model proposes or that was declared. The declared arbovirus → microcephaly test runs as a check of that component. The items below stand as confirmations and positives, not as the search.

1. **The distributed-lag term in the monolith** (an exposure field lifted to the outcome's places, β(ℓ) a GMRF over lag).
   - *2026-10-06:* `relations.distributed_lag` built in its two-stage form (the outcome model's μ as offset; NB; RW2 with τ by Laplace). On a planted curve with its mass at lags 5–9 (400 places × 120 months, φ 8) the window is lags 5–9 and the cumulative effect 0.424 ± 0.020 against 0.437. The window comes from a simultaneous band: per-lag 95 % intervals marked a lag in 22 of 100 null worlds, the band in 1. Over 100 worlds per effect size, the band found a window inside lags 5–9 in 73 at a peak effect of 0.05 and in 99 at 0.15. The monthly code-list reader (SINASC's CODANOMAL) waits on `_assemble`'s next change.
   - **Declared before running:** arbovirus → microcephaly (the lag curve's mass at 5–9 months, Q90 and the reverse direction as negative controls).
     - *Protocol, committed 2026-10-06 before any run:*
       - **Outcome:** SINASC CODANOMAL Q02 births by place and month, 2010–2023, with its expectation μ from the monthly anomaly fit (block XVII, B1).
       - **Exposure:** arbovirus notifications (SINAN DENG + CHIK + ZIKA) per 1,000 residents, as log(1 + rate). *Amended before any run on the outcome:* on a dengue self-test, a linear rate made exp(βx) explode in epidemic months.
       - **Grain:** both aggregated to immediate region × month.
       - **Model:** `relations.distributed_lag`, lags 0–12, φ from the anomaly fit; Northeast regions, and Brazil as a second reading.
       - **Pass:** the simultaneous-band window is non-empty, lies within lags 3–11, and the cumulative effect is positive, in the Northeast.
       - **Negative controls:** Q90 (Down syndrome) on the same exposure, and the reversed direction (the exposure led by the outcome: x(t + ℓ)); each passes when its window is empty.
   - Cold → respiratory admissions (RR 1.07 per the cited study) as the second positive.
2. **The shared-component model**, checked against the E_b screen's admitted pairs (infant mortality ↔ sanitation, Roemer's law).
3. **The endemic–epidemic term** for dengue, chikungunya and Zika between places.
4. **Every relation is checked on null worlds** with planted lag responses (§10.3).
