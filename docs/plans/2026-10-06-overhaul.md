# The overhaul (2026-10-06)

**ARCHITECTURE revision 2** (ADR-0023) says *what* PegaSUS is now. This file says *in what order it is built*, what each step must show before the next starts, and what is running. The reasons are in `docs/discussion/2026-10-06-architecture-review.md`.

## Rules for every package

- **One package at a time,** finished before the next starts: built, measured, an evaluation entry, a decision. Results that arrive from earlier runs are read when they land, inside the package they belong to.
- **Literature first (P11).** A package's first step names the established method for its estimand and its reference implementation (R or Python), and states whether PegaSUS's version must match it or beat it.
- **Benchmarked (P13).**
  - A solver change runs the benchmark (`bench`, O1).
  - A detection or relation method runs its slice of the planted grid before it is compared with what it replaces.
- **Machine discipline.** Heavy jobs go through `scripts/heavy.py`, and chains through `data/chain.py`. Few fat jobs, all cores. A job's watcher catches both its completion and its failure.

## O1: the solver

**Steps:**
1. **Assemble the Hessian from the factorised μ**, block by block (leaf-place diagonal, place blocks, the place–global coupling, the global block). Check it against autodiff Hessian–vector products on random vectors for SIM.DO VII, IX and a monthly SINAN block, to relative error 10⁻⁸.
2. **Eliminate in closed form, then factor:**
   - the leaf-place parameters eliminated in closed form;
   - the place system factored by sparse Cholesky (CHOLMOD through scikit-sparse, installed into `pegasus` and declared in `pyproject.toml`; SuperLU fallback);
   - the Schur complement on the globals, dense.
   - Constraints by contrast bases, with kriging for the ICAR components.
3. **Exact Newton** with the existing line search. A preconditioned-CG path on the exact product is used where the global block is large (monthly grain).
4. **BYM2:** one σ and the mixing ρ per graph term.
5. **LAML in log τ** (Newton or BFGS), its gradient by selected inversion. Fellner–Schall's fixed point is kept only as a cross-check of the optimum.
6. **Selected inversion and exact draws** replace `laplace.py`'s perturbation draws.
7. **The benchmark** (`bench`) over the four benchmark blocks of ARCHITECTURE §5.8, cold and warm.

**Accepted when:**
- the v0 optimum is reached on the benchmark blocks (objective within one log-likelihood unit, the τ's within tolerance, the same calibration);
- the budgets of §5.8 are met, or revised with the reason;
- the evaluation entry records v0 against v1 for every block.

**Then:** the v0 solver path is removed, and with it `_trace_inv_times`, `move_tol`'s ridge workaround, and Anderson on the fixed point. No second solver is kept beside the first.

## O3: characterise

1. **The planted grid of ARCHITECTURE §10.3** over real fitted fields of SIM, SIH, SINASC and SINAN, annual and monthly.
2. **Null worlds** (§10.4) and the existing negatives (§10.2).
3. **Re-make every v0 constant on the grid:** θ0 per lens, the trend's δ per scale, group sd, the alarm's history rule, the trend reference, and the triage thresholds (FAC_K, FAC_SHARE, ×1.6, z ≥ 3). The documented events are then scored, held out.
4. **IHW weights** (§8.4) from the power surface; every result's minimum detectable effect.
5. **Retire the gate in code:**
   - `tools.SURVEY_PLAN` runs every lens;
   - `gate` becomes `method`, a lead's record of its method;
   - `fields.admission` becomes the weight's covariate.

## O4: departure models
