# ADR-0019: Replication is artefact-aware: other jurisdictions, graded recording explanations, conservation, shape, the observed national rate

**Date.** 2026-10-05. **Status.** Active. Amends the spatial tier and the reading of unit claims of [ADR-0015](ADR-0015-replication-from-independent-units.md).

**Evidence.** `docs/evaluation/2026-10-05-replicated-claims-read.md` (and its correction): of the 57 state national-trend claims that held in later years *and* in the state's other municipalities, the aggregate data are consistent with a recording explanation for most, because a state's coding regime recurs in time and serves every municipality of the state. The tiers confirm regimes as readily as epidemiology. The coordinator's correction: a recording explanation read from aggregates (composition, sibling substitution, ill-defined to defined) is something the data *cannot exclude*, not an established recoding (the São Paulo "60% moved to undetermined intent" is an upper bound). Measured here: `docs/evaluation/2026-10-05-artefact-aware-replication.md` (of the 57: 13 not replicated on the observed national rate, 5 explained, 2 re-scoped, 15 open, 22 survive).

## Decision

1. **Every recording explanation carries an evidence grade** (`explain.GRADES`): **tested** (a test separates it from the claim: a marker at the level of the deaths, a facility step against stable volume elsewhere, a person-year check), **bound** (it can account for at most a stated share of the effect, under a stated assumption), **consistent** (the aggregates cannot exclude it, nothing more). **Only a tested explanation downgrades a claim**; bound and consistent keep it alive with the explanation attached. `explain.triage` grades each of its classes and `Session.triage` marks a lead `explained` only when the grade is tested; corroboration is offered to every lead not explained by a tested explanation.
2. **The spatial tier halves by the unit that sets the coding regime** (`replication.jurisdiction`): a *state* claim must hold in other states (more of them diverging the same way, on the same codes, than chance allows: binomial at 0.05 over the states with 30 or more events, at least two); otherwise its scope is *one jurisdiction* and it is not spatially confirmed (a regime, a local change and a model misfit cannot be told apart by that unit). A *region* claim is tested on a random half of its states (select 0.05, replicate 0.05 on the other) and without its most influential state (half the slope kept), else re-scoped to that state. A *municipality* claim, read against its neighbours, must not be carried by its state's other municipalities (else re-scoped to the state). ADR-0015's halves of the unit's own municipalities are removed (`spatial_split`, `test_spatial`, `simulate_spatial`).
3. **Direct standardisation on the year's observed national rate** (`replication.Strata`, `audit`): the claim's slope against the observed national rate by sex x age must reach the lens's minimum divergence in its direction with at least half the survey's size (`control.replicates`). A claim that fails is `not_replicated` and `kinds_of` gives it no confirmation: it was selected against the model's national course, which a code whose national rate moves fast misses.
4. **Conservation inside an ICD family** (`explain.exchange`): the rest of the node's ICD block, R00-R99 and, for external causes, undetermined intent Y10-Y34, and all of them pooled. A pool whose rest moves the other way by at least half the node's change in excess deaths, at 3 standard errors, raises a recording explanation of grade **bound** (at most that share, if every death the pool lost were the node's). It becomes **tested** by the individual-level profile of the displaced deaths (`explain.profile_test`): their sex, age, place of occurrence (the code's fourth character) and, between the intent chapters, mechanism (ICD-10 lists X85-Y09 and Y10-Y34 in one order: firearm X93-X95 / Y22-Y24, sharp object X99 / Y28 ...), compared with the node's and the pool's own by simulated log-likelihood ratio: node-like and not pool-like **supports** the recoding (tested), pool-like and not node-like **excludes** it (tested), both or neither leaves it a bound.
5. **Shape** (`explain.shape_test`): an abrupt one-year step against the better of a log-linear and a log-quadratic course (deviance gap in units of the dispersion >= `STEP_DELTA` = 6, calibrated on negative-binomial worlds). A step is classed *administrative*, grade **consistent**: a level shift is what a change of practice looks like, and a real shock too; only corroboration (a dated change, an independent record) can test it.
6. **The unit's all-cause slope** is a mandatory column of every claim (`audit["all_cause"]`); carrying half of the claim or more it raises a completeness-or-denominator co-movement of grade bound.
7. **Re-scope, never dissolve** (coordinator, 2026-10-05): when a pool took the code-level change (composition, sibling substitution, ill-defined to defined, intent reclassification) and no test excludes it, the claim is **lifted** to the level where the total is conserved (the node + its ICD block, R00-R99, undetermined intent) and re-tested there with the full statistics of a unit claim (`replication.conserved_level`: slope on the observed national rate against the scale's minimum, one-sided p, size over the period, later years, other jurisdictions). Both are reported: the conserved-level claim (epidemiology) and the code-level shift (certification, informative about data quality). A lead is dropped only when nothing remains at any conserved level.
8. **Verdicts** of `replication.audit`: `not_replicated`; `rescoped` (a tested explanation accounts for at least half at the code level and a conserved level stands); `explained` (nothing remains at any conserved level); `open` (explanations remain, none excluded by a test); `survives` (none raised, or every one excluded by a test). `Session.spatial_confirm` runs it for the unit claims of a register and writes `lead.robustness["artefact"]` (with `rescope`), `replications["national"]` and `replications["spatial_unit"]`; status `rescoped` or `explained` accordingly. Triage marks a lead `explained` only when its grade is tested.

## Limits

- Only unit claims are lifted automatically. A cell-level lead classed substitution or system keeps its grade and stays in the register; its family-level scan, and the re-test of a facility lead's residual once the facility's step is set aside, are not built.
- A lift that adds the R group is dominated by the R group's own course (the node's share of deaths is reported): it is a certification statement unless the receiving causes are included.
- The profile test needs displaced deaths (20 or more) and a profile that differs between node and pool; between two stroke codes sex and age tell little, and the grade stays *bound*. A tested exclusion is evidence against recoding *by that pool*, not against every recording explanation.
- The jurisdiction of a state claim is the state; whether a state has several coders (medico-legal institutes, regional secretariats) is not known to the data. A claim that holds in other states because the national course is misfit is re-scoped to the nation by the same test.
- Direct standardisation uses the nation, which contains the unit. The census-based person-years (POPSVS) are the denominator throughout; the account-3 check of the reading is not repeated.
- Thresholds (`EXCHANGE_SHARE` 0.5, `EXCHANGE_Z` 3, `STEP_DELTA` 6, `PROFILE_MIN` 20) were set from the simulations of the evaluation entry, not tuned on the 57 claims' labels.
