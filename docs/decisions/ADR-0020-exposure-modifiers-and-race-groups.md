# ADR-0020: Completeness, SUS-dependent exposure and race enter the expectation as exposure modifiers and race-stratified groups; opt-in until the refits are read

**Date.** 2026-10-05. **Status.** Active for the mechanism; the defaults are not changed (stage 1 proxy favours κ, stages 2 and 3 are built and not yet measured). Amended as each stage's fits land.

**Evidence.** `docs/evaluation/2026-10-05-exposure-41.md` (`data/exposure/kappa_proxy.py`, `scripts/measure_exposure.py`); pegasus_data decisions 0146 (system completeness, SUS-dependent), 0143, 0149 (race confusion), 0151 (population-account-3 with race).

## Decision

1. **κ and N^{(SUS)} are factors of the offset, not terms of η.** The population source string carries exposure modifiers: `popsvs+kappa`, `popsvs+sus`, `hybrid+kappa+sus`, `account-3+confusion` (`gateway.split_source`). `gateway.population(..., dataset=)` returns N' = κ_{s(dataset),UF(u),t} · q_{u,sex,band,t} · N with s' the quadrature of the parts, so μ = κ · N^{(v)} · exp(η) with η untouched, the exposure variance of ADR-0010 carries κ's and q's uncertainty, and the stored-fit keys name the modifiers and their model versions (`population`, `population_model`). A dataset without a completeness series (SIH, SINAN) raises on `+kappa` instead of reading 1 silently.
2. **κ** is pegasus_data's `system_completeness` (`system-completeness-2`: SIM and SINASC by UF × year 2000-2023, 80 % interval read as σ of log κ; basis published to 2011, then a calibrated extension). The model's geography and history absorb κ's UF level and national trend; what κ adds is the UF × year path (the North and Northeast rose by 5 to 9 points 2010-2023).
3. **N^{(SUS)}** is POPSVS times q, the share of the population without private coverage, from `sus-dependent-2` (municipality × sex × five-year band, **2021-2023 only**; a pooled comparable area gives each member its pooled share). Any other year reads the cell's 2021-23 mean, typed as extended: σ of log q grows as a random walk of the annual drift measured on 2021 to 2023 (population-weighted rms of the cells' log changes), so a share held fixed is uncertain, not observed (P8). **To extend backwards properly (pegasus_data work to propose):** ANS beneficiaries by municipality × age × sex, December 2008-2020 (the open-data PDA-024 starts 2021-05; the older series is ANS TabNet, not retrieved, DATA_SOURCES), through the same `sus_dependent.py`; until then SIH fits before 2021 rest on a frozen share and its time trend is carried by h(t).
4. **Race in the groups g** is built as **race-stratified blocks** (`race=` in the source arguments of `assemble`, `event_counts` and `Expectations`), not as a third axis of G, because η's age-sex profile is a random walk over G = 2 × bands and a race axis would change it (another front's ground). Each race is a block with its own θ, f, g, h. Sharing across races (one geography with race offsets) is not built (§13).
   - **Births:** by the mother's declared race (`RACACORMAE`, from 2012), exposure the account-3 race slice of women; no confusion (the declaration is the anchor). Blank race is unallocated, 'race unknown' (3.3 % of 2021 births).
   - **Infant deaths (SIM, chapter XVI):** by *recorded* race k, exposure `N^{rec}_k = Σ_j C(k|j,UF) · r_j · N_j` at age 0 (`+confusion`): C is `race_confusion_infant` averaged over the perinatal deaths' age mixture (0-6 d 0.70, 7-27 d 0.22, 28-364 d 0.08, read from the certificates of 2021-22), r_j the declared-race rate ratios fitted by Poisson maximum likelihood on UF × year recorded counts (`gateway.declared_ratios`, Branca = 1), N_j the account-3 race slice. So the expected recorded count is μ^{rec}_k = Σ_j C(k|j) μ_j when the declared races share one shape and differ by r_j (the architecture's formula under that restriction). From age 1 the matrix is not measured: recorded = declared. Recorded blank and the other races' deaths are not this block's events; blank is unallocated.
   - **Adult deaths stay recorded race:** the infant matrix does not transport (pegasus_data 0149) and the women's bridge covers women 15-49 only.
5. **Defaults.** `default_population` is unchanged for every field without a race (κ is not on by default: see the stage 1 numbers); a field with `race=` defaults to `account-3` (`account-3+confusion` for SIM.DO). The decision to switch κ on for SIM and SINASC waits for the refit evaluation (the stored fits `ix`, `births` × `popsvs+kappa`, queued behind the heavy slots).

## What the numbers say so far
Stage 1, refit-free on the stored POPSVS B1 fits: the UF × year residual of SIM chapter IX follows log κ (two-way centred slope 2.3, bootstrap 95 % over UFs 1.1 to 3.9, correlation 0.62, 378 cells); multiplying the expectation by κ (renormalised by UF level and year) lowers the negative log-likelihood by 306 (0.12 %), 308 of it in the North and Northeast, +53 in the Southeast. Births: κ barely varies after 2011, NLL -37 of 343,490. Stage 2 and stage 3 fits are not read.

## What it does not decide
Whether κ is right in level (the published ratio is the Ministry's and rests on 2008 Busca Ativa factors; census-based SINASC completeness is higher, pegasus_data 0146); a slope above 1 means the residual moves more than κ, so κ is a partial explanation or is correlated with another rising quantity (ill-defined causes). Whether q has trended since 2008. Whether the recorded-race exposure with r_j beats the declared-race exposure N_k on held-out recorded counts.
