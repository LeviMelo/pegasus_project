# What the May–July engine actually did

**2026-10-03.** This note corrects `RECOLLECTION.md` where it described specifications as if they were the running system.

Sources:
- four read-only audits of `LEVI/PegaSUS` (code, documents, commit history);
- RIPSA's technical note on POPSVS, read in full.

Every line below separates what was specified, what was built, what ran in the live path, and what was measured.

## Corrections to the recollection

- **The national pancreatic-cancer run covered 2021–2022 only** (T = 2). No lag could be estimated on it.
- **No state-level (Alagoas) run of the dependence model is documented.** Alagoas appears only in smoke tests and the unwritten Zika acceptance test.
- **The population tensor never used POPSVS.** It rebuilt, from census interpolation, what the Ministry publishes.

## The dependence engine (LDO)

**Gaussianisation.**
- Most variables got a randomised rank transform pooled over all municipalities × all years. That transform carries the spatial gradient and the time trend into the "standardised" value.
- Counts with exposure later got a negative-binomial or zero-inflated margin with a per-municipality empirical-Bayes rate. That path resolved **0 variables** nationally until a path bug was fixed. Afterwards it resolved 74 variables, and corr(z, log population) fell **0.93 → −0.05**.

**One model for everything.**
- One covariance, pooled over all places and 25 years, fed a sparse + low-rank precision fit (ADMM).
- Temporal whitening existed but was off. The space × time Kronecker form was telemetry only.
- **The low-rank penalty had no stable value:**
  - at λ₂ ≥ 0.2 the shared factor collapsed into a clique of direct edges;
  - at λ₂ ≤ 0.05 the low-rank part absorbed everything.
  
  Production hard-coded λ₂ = 1.0.

**Reliability weights.** Computed throughout the pipeline. The estimator used them only late, and partially.

## The residual scan and the national artefact

The scan: HSIC on residuals, all pairs, a permutation null, Benjamini–Yekutieli FDR. National run: **7,197 "nonlinear" edges** forming a near-clique. Causes, in the order found:
1. **A wrong first diagnosis** (low-rank leak). The fix was correct, but it moved nothing on the real data.
2. **No conditioning on place and time.** A two-way fixed effect removed most of the clique. But the municipality effect erases between-place signal, so **the scan silently became a within-municipality, over-time test.**
3. **A haze of negligible dependence** (CKA ≈ 0.04) certified by huge n. **The effect-size floor was the strongest lever: 696 → 84 edges.**
4. **A degenerate sample.** A greedy variable rule dropped n from 1,599 to 145 singleton cells, which pinned CKA at **1.0 for every pair**.
5. **Multiplicity arithmetic.** With a permutation floor of 1/1,001 and BY over 60,000 pairs, the scan certifies either nothing or about 7,000 edges at once.

**After the fixes:** a 43-variable São Paulo slice gave 22 certified edges, all of them textbook. **The national file was never rerun.**

**The finding lenses.**
- They ran on raw panels, each with its own FDR, with no global error control.
- Only the space-time scan had a real null.
- "Stratum concentration" compared strata with uniform means, not with population shares.

## The population tensor

- **Inputs:** census strata (SIDRA 9606, 2093), totals (SIDRA 6579), and civil-registry births and deaths (2609, 2683).
- **In the default mode survival was 1** (nobody died). Deaths were absorbed into "migration".
- **Intercensal race, age and sex composition** was a linear interpolation of census shares. One closure constraint per municipality-year sat against about 1,200 unknowns.
- **Totals** were geometric interpolations of censuses. They overwrote 6579, including the post-2022 rebased years.
- **Uncertainty:** a flat 0.02. Loss weights were hand-set, and the solver was capped at 12 iterations.
- **No intercensal validation:** no leave-one-census-out test was ever run. Census years match by construction.

## Reconstruction of context

- The ST-DFM never received a validation mask, so it always reported "uncertified".
- **No reconstructed context reached the analysis.** Context entered as observed cells with zero weight elsewhere.
- The regime classifier ran with three of its conditions hard-coded to true.

## The race bridge

- **The live path was the identity.** Missing race was spread 1/5 to each category.
- **The ecological estimator was built but never called outside tests.** Its form:
  - a free 25-logit confusion matrix with a weak pull to a soft identity;
  - no age or sex terms;
  - no literature prior;
  - no ordinal structure, so **the "small, structured C" hypothesis was never tested.**
- **Its validation was circular:** it was tested only on data generated by its own model class.
- It was refused on Alagoas (composition spread 0.0015 < 0.005) and never run nationally.
- The national pancreatic study divided raw administrative race by census race. Its report says "bridged".

## The measure algebra (EFG)

- 12 operators declared; 4 used (count, record functional, ratio, divergence).
- "Support" was a free-form dict, and the alignment check was vacuous. The real alignment was the ratio kernel's join: it sums the numerator to the denominator's geography and refuses cross-joins.
- Live variable sets were about 150–350 fields.
- The utility score, θ_compile and the empirical compression stage were never built.

## POPSVS (RIPSA Technical Note 01/2025, doi:10.5281/zenodo.17990183)

**Method.**
- The Duchesne cohort-ratio method (Freire, Gonzaga & Gomes 2020), anchored on the 2000, 2010 and 2022 census sector aggregates by municipality, sex and age.
- Constrained to IBGE's 2024 state projections by sex and age (cohort-component), and to IBGE's official municipal totals of 1 July.
- Growth is estimated in two levels: intermediate region against its state, municipality against its intermediate region. Periods 2000–2010 and 2012–2022, with a mid-period estimate, then interpolation.

**Content.** Single ages 0–79, then 80+; both sexes; 2000–2025; municipalities compatibilised over territorial changes.

**Its stated limitation:** small-area shocks between censuses (economic, migratory, environmental) are smoothed over.

**In pegasus_data:** POPSVS is what TabNet divides by (27 of 27 states equal in 2022). Its 2022 total, 210,862,983, is 3.8% above the census enumeration because of the adjustment to IBGE's projections and official totals.
