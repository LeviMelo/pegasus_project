## 1. What went wrong, at the root

1. **No document owns a kind of fact, so every document drifts.** The same state lives in STATUS, the coverage matrix, ARCHITECTURE §13, plan status lines and commit messages. Of 27 commits since the morning review, 3 touched ARCHITECTURE, 1 added an evaluation, none an ADR; check_docs and ruff passed throughout, because they check form, not content.
2. **The author's rules live in memory and conversation.** Of 38 rules and goals the author has stated, 14 are in no canonical document (never name variables; no ritual reconciliation; no development agents; no doc-only commits; plan interfaces with the author; pre-1996 low priority; standing download approval; and others). Eight pairs of rules contradict each other across documents (§4).
3. **Goals that decide daily work were never written:** who reads PegaSUS, what a finished reading is, what a reading may cost, what counts as success, what is out of scope.
4. **The architecture's use model is "always the whole survey"** (ARCHITECTURE §9.3: each update refits, re-surveys, re-scans), and §5.8 budgets only the fits. Nothing bounds or shows the cost of a reading.
5. **My conduct.** S1 to S5 landed in 16 minutes, each without a run; S1's own acceptance (documented positives for a measure and a composition) was never met before S2–S6 began; a new constant (`LOCATION_EFFECT`) was set by hand; a second noise estimator was built beside N1; claims of "done" and "code complete" were made of untested work; heavy runs were queued by a mechanism beside `scripts/heavy.py`; after the context summary the plans were not re-read.

## 2b. What a reading costs (survey 3)

The survey path is maximal by default and has almost no cost settings. `cli survey` takes `--blocks` and `--levels`; a plan adds `questions`, per-system `levels` and which declared readers run. Nothing chooses fields, methods, replicates or years per system, and `ask(**kw)` sends one setting to every method (a method lacking it records a silent failure).

| object | budget (§5.8) | measured |
|---|---|---|
| SIH chapter X annual fit | 2 min | 71 s |
| 16 SIH chapters, warm | 30 min for 20 | 1,013 s |
| chapter IX, cold | 20 s | 35 s |
| a chapter survey | under 2 min | 136 s per SIM group field (3 questions); 893 s per SIH X field (7 questions); SIH X groups: 8.4 h |
| triage and corroboration of 16,806 leads | — | under 5.5 min |

Where the time goes, per field (profile `data/logs/prof_questions.pstats`, SIH X J09–J18, 893 s):
- `surprise.spatial_structure` 189 s: four calls of 47 s, three of them identical (excess, step, trend at tier B1), about 94 s repeated.
- `share` 427 s, of which 280 s assembled all 20 chapters and loaded 20 robust fits (fixed for the annual path, a6b8303); the beta-binomial θ search still costs 105 s (51 scipy evaluations).
- Spectrum (27 s) and scales (8 s) rebuilt in every `Session`, one per declared reader; `_shape` runs `series` and `attribute` per finding (21.6 s); institutions bypass the surprise memo (about 15 s); the ledger writes one parquet file per call.

Hidden expensive defaults:
- Reading a count block runs `robust_stored`: about 15 min per chapter when no robust fit is stored (six ran inside the first group of the SIH X update).
- The assembly key hashes gateway.py's bytes; gateway.py changed in 31 of 263 commits since 10-04, each orphaning 658 assemblies (4.2 GB).
- `_expected_total` fits a missing national `*` model silently; `event_counts` may download up to 8 GiB on a cache miss.
- `update` keys every step by the repository commit (any commit redoes everything), writes the register once at the end with no per-field checkpoint, and multiplies the whole survey by each declared reader and by `confirm_last`.
- The job runner (`scripts/heavy.py`, `data/chain.py`) is used by nothing in the package; 17 `data/queue_*.py` loops exist beside it; the GPU is taken with no slot; no thread counts are set.

What configurability would touch: the Bonferroni factor k (the number of methods per field, questions.py), the stored method records and the ledger's FDR families (a reading of fewer methods is a different family); the replicate counts, which set the smallest attainable p-value (peaks 40, scan 200 to 2,000, corroboration 4,999 to 99,999); stage E, which reads `provenance.by_method`; and the cache keys, of which none exists for (field, question, method, settings).

## 4. Rulings for the author

1. **pegasus_data.** Developed from this session under its own CLAUDE.md (as STATUS says), or only through handoffs (as ARCHITECTURE §2 rule 2 says)? *Recommendation:* directly, under pegasus_data's discipline; §2 amended.
2. **pegasus_view.** Never touched (memory, 2026-10-05) and pegasus_data's CLAUDE.md "keep it working, change both repositories": *recommendation:* both hold — PegaSUS's work never touches pegasus_view; pegasus_data keeps the API pegasus_view reads stable.
3. **Who decides what.** "Decide, no menu" and "ask before reshaping use" both stand. *Recommendation:* a table in CLAUDE.md §4: the agent decides methods, fixes and documentation; the author decides goals, use and cost (§9.3), interfaces and serving, scope and priority, and anything outward.
4. **Use and cost (§9.3).** How a reading is chosen and bounded. The options, which are not exclusive:
   - (a) *A reading is named, not implied:* fields, questions, methods and years chosen in the plan or the call, defaults small (one field, the cheap methods); the whole survey becomes one named reading among others.
   - (b) *Cost shown before it is spent:* every reading estimates its fits, assemblies, downloads and method calls from what is stored, and refuses past a budget unless told otherwise (as links already are, `PEGASUS_COMPUTE_LINKS`).
   - (c) *Incremental by content:* keys by the inputs and the code a step reads, not the commit; one checkpoint per field and question; a stored result reused when its key holds.
   - (d) *Fewer repeats first:* `spatial_structure`, the spectrum and the robust fit computed once per field or session; this needs no ruling and only changes speed.
   *Recommendation:* (d) now as speed work, (c) next, (a) and (b) designed with the author, because they change what a finding means (the FDR family and the method records depend on which methods ran).
5. **Goals (a new ARCHITECTURE §1.2).** A draft of the reader, a finished reading, the cost of a reading, success, and non-goals, for the author to correct; goals are theirs to set.
6. **Today's field kinds.** Keep them, recorded as v0 with their debts and no new kind until S1's acceptance is met on real data (*recommendation*), or revert those without a check.
