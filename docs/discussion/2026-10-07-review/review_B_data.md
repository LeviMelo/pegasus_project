Audit 2026-10-07, read-only. Sources: pegasus_data README/STATUS/DECISIONS (ADR-0001..0153), `__init__.py` (66 lazy exports), `curation/roles/*.yml` (loaded through `load_column_roles()`), `load_event_types()` (67 event types), `curation/links.yml`, `curation/fields.yml`, lake listings; pegasus_core `src/pegasus_core/**` greps for `pegasus_data`/`pg.`.

## 2. Unused capabilities ranked by leverage

1. **Record linkage (`link`, `build_entities`, `p_match`, `link_draws`)**: the only way to attribute events to persons across SIH, SIM, SINASC, CIHA; 91% of in-hospital deaths linked at <1.1% FDR. pegasus_core has a cohort scan waiting for `draws` (scans/cohort.py) but no gateway call.
2. **Care flows (`care_flows`, catchments, communities) and REGIC graphs**: model residence-vs-facility leakage in SIH, the main artefact class for place-level SIH leads; nothing in pegasus_core reads them.
3. **Aggregates (`aggregate`, lake municipality-month tables incl. 49 SINAN agravos)**: replaces per-year `count_events` re-reads; SIM national 2010-2023 reproduces TabNet 14/14.
4. **Climate field (INMET, 1961-2025)**: exposure for every vector/water-borne agravos; pegasus_core's contexts (map_inputs.py) have no weather.
5. **Event types not yet fitted: CIHA hospitalisation** (+~17% of SIH volume, ADR-0137 and DATA_SOURCES) and **SIM-DOFET**: both declared, nothing fitted.
6. **SIA APAC authorisations (AQ, AR, ATD, AN)**: declared event types with roles, 4 datasets, zero pegasus_core use; adds chemo, radio, dialysis, nephrology as a utilisation block.
7. **Roles beyond strata**: ~90% of 1,540 columns are marks/dimensions; unlocks "a system newly declared yields more than counts" (docs/plans/2026-10-07-fields-from-roles.md).
8. **`race_reliability()` and `race_confusion_women`**: currently only the infant matrix is used; adult race terms and SIH race flags (329 hospital-months) missing.
9. **`availability()` / `field_coverage()`**: replaces hand-written windows; guards structural zeros per field/year.
10. **Query dimensions and CNES enrichment (17 attributes as of month)**: hospital type/beds per record for institution lenses without the facility.py hand join.
11. **Remaining 29 of 35 CNES supply fields**: CT, MRI, dialysis, CHA hours, non-SUS beds, establishment counts.
12. **Population account -5/-6 (1991-1999, migration-schedule timing)**: pegasus_core reads accounts 2/3/4 only (gateway.py `TENSOR`); `account-6` is referenced only for `hybrid`.
13. **SIDRA registry/fact store**: replaces hand-named IBGE fields; state-level PNS/PNAD not built in pegasus_data either.
14. **Snapshots (versions of revised preliminary files)**: direct evidence for reporting-delay models.
15. **ICD-9 bridge**: SIM 1979-1995 into the ICD tree with comparability uncertainty; low priority per owner.
