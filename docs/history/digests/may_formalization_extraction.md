- **Ecological lattice Ω / L = S×T(×A)**: the municipality×time(×stratum) support all variables are defined over. [Docs 1,3,4,5]
- **Typed field / typed cube**: an OLAP cube or DATASUS-derived field carrying a type signature (kind, carrier, unit, support, aggregation law, role, provenance) that gates legal algebra. [Docs 1,2,3,4]

- **Pushforward/Marginalization (π_*)**: OLAP roll-up; sums an additive measure over a dropped dimension; invalid for medians/rates. [Docs 1,2,3,4,5]
- **Pullback (π\*)**: broadcasts a coarse contextual field to a finer support; explicitly illegal as a denominator-generator without a supplied allocation/disintegration kernel (that belongs to Problem 3). [Docs 1,2,3,4]

- **Absolute continuity (ν≪μ)**: support-compatibility/definability condition for the RN derivative; explicitly NOT a causal claim. [Docs 1,2,4]
- **Dimensional/denominator-compatibility predicate**: type-level rule blocking nonsensical ratios (e.g. deaths/GDP) while allowing canonical ones (deaths/population). [Docs 1,2,4]
- **Align-first-divide-second rule**: numerator and denominator measures must be pushed to a common target support before dividing, to avoid ecological weighting artifacts. [Doc 2, formalized in doc 4]
- **Type Signature Γ**: minimal (kind, carrier, unit, support, aggregation-law, role, provenance) tuple gating legal operations without clinical semantic bloat. [Docs 1,2,3,4]

- **Canonical Core vs Exploratory Space**: forced, pruning-immune generation of standard epidemiological indicators vs a freely explored/pruned combinatorial space. [Docs 1,2,3,4,5]
- **Semantic Entropy / cross-domain amalgamation penalty**: soft regularizer discouraging highly semantically mixed composite variables unless statistically exceptional. [Doc 1]
- **Deferred (terminal-only) equivalence-class collapse**: collinearity-based variable merging performed once, at the end, right before the scanner — never mid-generation, to preserve combinatorial potential. [Docs 1,2]
