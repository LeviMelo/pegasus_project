3. SETTLED / AGREED CONCLUSIONS STATED BY THE DOCUMENT
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════

**(a) What an ecological-link engine can/cannot identify:**
- Lag-extended precision-matrix edges are conditional-GRANGER-predictive associations, NOT directed causal effects (LDO-LAG-CONF-01, DIRECT-LAG-01) — temporal precedence rules out reverse causation but not common-trend confounding.
- Kronecker separability (one global Ω_var, one global Σ_space) structurally FORBIDS space-varying/lag-varying dependence, which is exactly the epidemiologically interesting case for spatially heterogeneous disease coupling (LDO-SEP-08) and for spatial-diffusion mechanisms of communicable disease (LDO-LAG-KRONSEP-10).
- Municipal-level direct age-standardization is stated (citing the system's own §III.3) to be the wrong tool for small areas — SAE/BYM/INLA shrinkage is the MSD's own prescribed and unapplied fix (ASTD-SAE-02).
- The intercensal population/race composition is, in the non-informative regime, mathematically the PRIOR (not a posterior informed by data) — the document states this as a strict identifiability fact (POP-IDENT-01, RACE-IDENTIFY-07), not merely a concern.
- Collider/v-structure orientation and non-Gaussianity-licensed LiNGAM orientation both rest on assumptions (faithfulness; genuine structural non-Gaussian noise) that the document argues are either untestable or actively falsified by panel-pooling artifacts (LDO-CAUSAL-FAITHFUL-07, LDO-CAUSAL-LINGAM-POOL-05).
- MAUP/ecological-fallacy-adjacent finding: coarse-to-fine reliability-weighted MEAN pooling across a UF is argued to be exactly the Simpson/cancellation-inducing aggregation operation the system's own anti-Simpson screen exists to catch (LDO-EXH-04, LDO-EXH-08, LDO-EXH-09) — i.e., the safeguard is undone by its own preprocessing step.

**(b) Multiplicity/selective inference:**
- Stated repeatedly across independent subsystems that stability selection as implemented does NOT deliver a Meinshausen-Bühlmann-style finite-sample error bound (wrong subsample fraction/construction, two mismatched thresholds — LDO-CERT-STABTHRESH-06), and no FDR/BH/BY layer is applied at several points where it is claimed as the multiplicity control (LDO-CERT-MULTITEST-07, LDO-LAG-MULTTEST-12, LDO-EXH-07, LDO-CAUSAL-MHT-14).
- Where BH/BY IS applied (HSIC residual scan), the document argues the underlying p-values violate even BY's assumptions because they share the same fitted precision matrix and the same reused permutation set (LDO-HSIC-05), and separately that the permutation-resolution floor combined with BY's harmonic-inflation makes detection structurally impossible at national variable counts (LDO-HSIC-06).
- The disease-hierarchy prior's penalty discount is argued to break the exchangeability stability selection's guarantee depends on (LDO-DIS-STAB-14).

**(c) Spatial/temporal dependence and effective sample size:**
- Consistent finding: the temporal axis of the claimed Kronecker-separable precision model is NEVER actually whitened in the estimator (only computed as unused telemetry) — LDO-TIME-03, LDO-LAG-OVERLAP-05. This is called out as CRITICAL because it means every lag-extended precision fit treats serially-autocorrelated overlapping windows as iid.
- The Moran's-I-based effective-n correction is found to be computed on a meaningless proxy (1-D tensor-cell-order, not real geography) in one code path (MORAN-ORDERING-PROXY-01, A20/DIRECT-QT-01), and even where geography is used, on raw unstandardized values dominated by population density rather than risk clustering (MORAN-VALUE-NOT-RATE-STD-01); and the deflation formula itself is stated to double-count dependence the GMRF already models (MORAN-DOUBLECORRECT-01).
- The document's single strongest cross-cutting factual conclusion: computed uncertainty/effective-sample-size quantities (W, n_eff, denom_fragility) are consistently NOT propagated into the estimators that need them — stated as one architectural defect spanning at least A1, A13, A20, A21, A22, LDO-MARG-05, LDO-LAG-NEFF-13, NEFF-KISH-WEIGHT-01.

**(d) Denominators and measurement error:**
- The document states as settled that the intercensal population denominator's own reconstruction uncertainty is dropped when used as a margin offset (LDO-MARG-08, MQ-EXPOSURE-VARIANCE-01) — a "classical/errors-in-variables" measurement-error problem in the offset that is acknowledged elsewhere in the spec (§II.4) but not implemented.
- Migration volume is stated as FORMALLY UNIDENTIFIED from net marginals alone (this is presented as the MSD's own admitted position, POP-GRAV-05) — the document's critique is that the resulting flow field's magnitude is then set entirely by an uncalibrated constant (base_rate=0.01) and reported as if estimated.
- Standardized-rate confidence intervals are computed (Fay-Feuer) but never propagated into the inference layer (ASTD-CI-PROP-15) — stated as a hard architectural gap, not a subtle one.

**(e) HSIC nonlinear dependence testing failure modes at scale:**
- In-sample (non-cross-fitted) residual HSIC is explicitly flagged as violating the system's OWN written cross-fitting mandate (LDO-HSIC-01) — this is presented as a contradiction of the spec by the implementation, not merely a weakness.
- At national scale, the memory-bound fallback (mean-pooling residuals to UF×bucket) is stated to make HSIC collapse to an approximately-linear statistic via CLT (LDO-HSIC-04) — and this coarsened path is asserted to be the COMMON national-scale path, not a rare edge case.
- The permutation null used is explicitly named as different from — and weaker than — the null regime the code's own metadata claims to have run (LDO-HSIC-02, LDO-HSIC-03) — described as provenance being "falsified."

**(f) What outputs are honest to emit:**
- The document repeatedly distinguishes "the system silently fabricates/asserts" vs. "the system honestly flags as approximate/descriptive" — e.g., praising the ADMM convergence flag's existence while arguing its consequence (auto-downgrade to "descriptive") is inadequate because an unconverged S/L split can be qualitatively (not just uncertainly) wrong (LDO-ADMM-06/DIRECT-CPW-02).
- Several findings explicitly invoke the system's own stated "never silently..." / anti-silent-fiction principles (from MSD/prime-directive language) to argue current behavior is a CONTRACT VIOLATION, not merely a statistical weakness: ASTD-AGEMISS-01 ("missingness with silence"), ASTD-POISSON-03 ("false precision"), LDO-DIS-OVERLAP-10 (mandatory §5.3 abort silently bypassed), LDO-EXH-12 (audit failure indistinguishable from success).
- The certification gate's own exact-vs-approximate check (§V.6(3)) is argued to certify the wrong thing (method fidelity, not scope validity — LDO-CERT-SLICE-SCOPE-04) and to be blind to the most damaging failure mode, false-positive edges invented by the approximation (LDO-CERT-BAND-ZERO-12).

4. CONCRETE METHODS RECOMMENDED vs REJECTED
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════

**Recommended (with reasons given in-document):**
- Negative-Binomial / hurdle-ZINB margins with estimated dispersion, replacing hardcoded Poisson — because Brazilian municipal counts are overdispersed and zero-inflated (LDO-MARG-01, LDO-MARGIN-10, LDO-DIS-SPARSE-07, LDO-CAUSAL-COUNT-DIST-13).
- Higham's alternating-projection nearest-correlation (or `statsmodels.corr_nearest`) instead of one-shot eigen-clip — because the current method isn't guaranteed PSD/unit-diagonal and distorts exactly the small eigenvalues that drive the precision (A7/DIRECT-WHIT-02, LDO-NCORR-02, LDO-LAG-NEARCORR-07).
- Weighted/Hajek-weighted moments and ECDFs using the already-computed reliability weight W, instead of unweighted — to actually realize the MSD's stated (and currently unconsumed) reliability architecture (A1, LDO-MARG-05, LDO-MARG-11, LDO-LAG-NEFF-13).
- Stability-selection-over-a-λ-path (StARS) or EBIC/CV for λ1/λ2 selection, instead of fixed magic constants — because a single fixed λ does not inherit stability selection's insensitivity guarantee (A8/DIRECT-CPW-01, LDO-LAMBDA-09).
- Genuine Meinshausen-Bühlmann construction (complementary-pair subsampling at exactly 1/2, PFER-derived threshold) OR explicit abandonment of the MB claim in favor of a stated heuristic (LDO-CERT-STABTHRESH-06).
- Cross-fitted (K-fold, spatial+temporal block-preserving) HSIC residuals, per the system's own §6.6.1 mandate — replacing in-sample residuals (LDO-HSIC-01).
- Spatial-block cyclic time-shift permutation null (already implemented in `nulls.py` but unused) instead of the executed weaker intra-UF shuffle — to actually achieve the exchangeability the registry claims (LDO-HSIC-02, LDO-HSIC-03).
- Benjamini-Yekutieli (dependence-robust FDR) preferred over BH given the shared-fit/shared-permutation dependence structure of HSIC p-values; Westfall-Young maxT/step-down or knockoffs/e-values as more rigorous alternatives valid under arbitrary dependence (LDO-HSIC-05, LDO-EXH-07).
- Conformal selection / knockoff filters to convert the exhaustiveness screen's unbacked "bounded false negative" assertion into an actual distribution-free guarantee, using the already-computed audit sample as calibration (LDO-EXH-14, echoed as LDO-CERT-ENH-CONFORMAL-14 for the certification gate more broadly).
- Subsampling/conformal edge-uncertainty construction built from the ALREADY-COMPUTED stability-subsample ensemble, replacing the analytic Fisher-z SE + separate SVD-error term + separate stability threshold with ONE internally-consistent object (LDO-CERT-ENH-CONFORMAL-14).
- Negative-binomial/Poisson-lognormal BYM2/INLA-SPDE small-area shrinkage BEFORE the copula, so certification can't lock onto population-size-driven stable-but-spurious noise patterns (LDO-CERT-ENH-NB-15, ASTD-SAE-02).
- Adaptive/Boyd-style ADMM ρ with primal+dual residual balancing and a real convergence certificate, instead of fixed ρ + primal-only stopping (A9/DIRECT-CPW-02, LDO-ADMM-06).
- Actual Kronecker temporal whitening (or GLS/autocorrelation-corrected effective-n) instead of leaving Σ_time as telemetry-only/identity (LDO-TIME-03, LDO-LAG-OVERLAP-05).
- SPDE/BYM2-style normalized spatial precision parameterization (Simpson 2017, Riebler 2016) instead of the unnormalized combinatorial Laplacian, to remove the degree-confounded whitening strength (LDO-WHITEN-04).
- Region-stratified/non-separable low-rank correction to the Kronecker model, treating separability as a TESTABLE hypothesis rather than backbone (LDO-SEP-08).
- Segmented Poisson/NB GLMs with log-population offset for ITS/DiD, replacing OLS-on-Gaussianized-mean; Bai-Perron multi-break detection with anticipation/phase-in windows instead of single-break argmax; Callaway-Sant'Anna / two-way-fixed-effects DiD with clustered SEs and pre-trends test (LDO-CAUSAL-ITS-GAUSS-01/02/03, LDO-CAUSAL-ITS-SINGLEBREAK-10, LDO-CAUSAL-COUNT-DIST-13, LDO-CAUSAL-DID-ORPHAN-15).
- Lipsitch/Tchetgen-Tchetgen-style calibrated negative-control-outcome procedure (empirical null across pre-declared true negative controls, Schuemie-style empirical calibration) instead of a raw fraction≥0.5 veto (LDO-CAUSAL-NC-BINOM-04).
- Fisher-z conditional-independence tests at controlled level using proper n_eff, FDR/Bonferroni-corrected across triples, instead of magnitude thresholds for collider detection; Meek-rule propagation for a consistent CPDAG instead of last-write-wins (LDO-CAUSAL-COLLIDER-THRESH-08/CONFLICT-09).
- Fixed-effects removal (de-mean within municipality/time) before non-Gaussianity licensing in LiNGAM, or panel-aware VAR-LiNGAM (LDO-CAUSAL-LINGAM-POOL-05).
- IPF/raking (multiplicative) closure instead of Euclidean simplex projection (subtract-and-clip), to preserve composition and avoid zeroing small strata (POP-CLOSURE-03).
- Leave-one-census-out temporal hold-out for hyperparameter tuning, instead of scoring against census-year cells only (POP-VALTUNE-14, LDO-CERT-HOLDOUT-NULL-10 analog).
- Rogers-Castro age-migration-schedule prior on migration composition, instead of uniform broadcast across strata (POP-AGGREG-11, POP-MIGBOUND-06).
- Cohort-component intercensal interpolation (age forward with survival+births / backward-age blend) instead of linear share interpolation (INTERP-COHORT-11).
- Bayesian/multiplicative zero-replacement before ILR race-composition transform, instead of a flat 1e-12 epsilon floor (POP-RACEILR-12, RACE-ILR-14).
- Literature-calibrated (PNAD/PCERP) race emission matrix C with a properly-derived Dirichlet concentration, and genuine inf/sup partial-identification bounds over the credible set, instead of concentration=250 fabricated tightness and a forbidden value·(1±w) heuristic (RACE-PRIOR-08).
- Clinically-grounded ontology distance (SNOMED-CT, MedDRA/phecode, Disease Ontology) or curated causal-chain KB edges (e.g., arbovirus→congenital anomaly) blended with/replacing raw ICD chapter/block adjacency as the disease-similarity prior (LDO-DIS-TREE-01, LDO-DIS-CHAPTER-08).
- Full AHRQ CCSR crosswalk (genuine multi-label 1-6 categories) or dropping CCSR for CID-10 entirely in favor of WHO-exact + curated Brazilian lists, instead of a primary-category-only reduction presented as multi-label (LDO-DIS-PROV-11).
- Sparse (scipy.sparse) Kronecker + sparse power-iteration Lipschitz bound for the disease smoothness operator, instead of dense Kron + dense `eigvalsh` (LDO-DIS-QUAD-DENSE-15).

**Rejected / criticized as currently implemented (not full method rejections, but flagged as inadequate substitutes):**
- Kish effective-n formula (rejected AS APPLIED to outcome values/denominators rather than true sampling weights; a design-effect from spatial ICC is preferred) — NEFF-KISH-WEIGHT-01, A21/DIRECT-QT-02.
- The `1/(1+MoranI)` n_eff deflation formula (rejected as undereived and double-counting; either drop it or replace with a derived VIF) — MORAN-DOUBLECORRECT-01.
- Fixed-threshold "sensitive filter" screening (LDO-EXH-01/02) explicitly rejected as NOT delivering the "tuned for recall" guarantee claimed — recommends a calibrated within-panel permutation null or variance-components (meta-analytic) heterogeneity statistic instead.
- Sign-only, un-nulled temporal-holdout "persistence rate" rejected as uninformative given a ~50%-by-chance floor; recommends permutation-null-calibrated, detrended, quantitative (not sign-only) agreement (LDO-CERT-HOLDOUT-NULL-10).
- The 3-point λ-grid regularization-path-agreement vote is rejected as too coarse; recommends a dense log-spaced path scored by persistence-interval length, with monotone-death vs non-monotone-flicker distinguished (LDO-CERT-PATHGRID-09).
- Fixed geometric (constant-rate) extrapolation for population closure beyond census anchors is rejected as unbounded/divergent for high-growth municipalities; recommends damped/logistic growth blending toward regional rates (POP-GEOM-13, INTERP-GEOM-12).

No mentions were found in the document of: e-values, standard conformal prediction being ADOPTED (only recommended as an enhancement, not already in use), or reduced-rank spatial-confounding-specific methods by that name — the closest analogs are the SPDE/BYM2 and INLA recommendations listed above, which the document repeatedly invokes as the MSD's own already-stated (but unimplemented) prescription for small-area / spatial confounding problems.
