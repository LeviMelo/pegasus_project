# The level of the prospective tier BP: place drift, the training fit's φ_extra, and regimes (2026-10-05)

**Scenario.** `scripts/measure_bp_level.py annual|dengue ORIGINS` and `scripts/bp_level_table.py` (artefacts `data/logs/bp_level_<kind>_<origin>.json`, local; fits by `scripts/fit_blocks.py`). **Regime.** design-v0, contiguity fits as cached in `pegasus_home/monolith`; MAP predictive; each variant scores the same test cells (the five years after the origin) as a mixture of NBs. Annual: SIM.DO deaths, seven fields (IX, I20-I25, I60-I69, I64, I10-I15, X, XVIII) at origins 2014 (test 2015–19), 2016 (XVIII not fitted; 2017–21) and 2019 (2020–23): 20 field-origin pairs. Monthly: dengue probable cases at origins 2014 and 2018. **Counted.** The held-out log score (sum over cells, higher is better), the PIT's KS pooled over the pairs, the mean per-pair KS and worst-region KS (§6.2), obs/expected.

**Where the miss lies** (oracle decompositions, chapter IX at 2019): rescaling each year to the observed national total moves KS 0.126 → 0.101, each macro-region-year 0.094, **each place's multiplier over the four years 0.013** (log score −79.5k → −67.6k). Dengue at 2018: the national-month oracle moves the log score −3.54M → −2.31M, the place multiplier −1.26M. BP's miscentring is the place's level, and for dengue the epidemic regime; the national h is the smaller part for deaths.

- **The gain holds at every origin:** damped h + course 0.5 against the reference, log score −562,340 → −510,814 (2014, the only origin whose test years precede COVID), −488,047 → −445,475 (2016), −467,216 → −417,540 (2019); mean KS .122 → .052, .135 → .085, .106 → .048. The pooled PIT is flat (deciles .098–.111 at 2014, outer deciles .112 and .116 at 2019).
- **The h forecast is second-order:** linear, damped and flat differ by under 700 nats once the course is in (−1,378,468 / −1,373,829 / −1,374,243); the damped slope is best by log score and was adopted. A random-walk spread on the national level adds 385 nats of 1.4 M: not adopted, and the earlier forecast-variance heuristic (`laplace.forecast_variance`) is removed.
- **The place trend needs damping:** at full slope the log score is 25,000 below that at 0.5; 0.25 and 0.5 are the same.
- **Not met:** the §6.2 criterion (KS ≤ 0.03, ≤ 0.05 in every region) is missed in the worst region (mean .116); regional departures remain.
- **Chapter I (infectious) is not a calibration case:** at 2019 B34 (COVID-19) is a category the fit never saw, KS .53 whatever the variant (obs/exp 3.7); over the three origins the pooled KS stays .27 although the log score rises −1.11 M → −0.66 M.

- Per origin: 2018 KS/worst .162/.264 → .073/.178, obs/exp 2.84 → 1.33; 2014 .177/.264 → .039/.060.
- **Most of dengue's "level" miss was the missing place-year component** (the block's φ is 0.27; log score −2.89 M → −1.02 M at 2018 from φ_extra alone), the rest the epidemic regime: the national year totals of the fit span 0.24 to 1.62 M and the five test years 0.54 to 1.64 M, so the fit's years form an ensemble that contains the future's range.
- **What remains:** Sul at 2018 (obs/exp 6.6, dengue's spread into the south, a place-level drift the history cannot contain; top PIT decile .166). A place trend on monthly cells over-extrapolates epidemic years (full slope: obs/exp 0.20; damped 0.5: 1.02, +6,000 nats of 780,000): not adopted.
- **Not run here:** dengue origins 2016 and 2020 and SIM origins 2018 and 2021: see "Follow-up" below.

## Verdict

BP's predictive was miscentred because it was too narrow and too still: the block's φ without the place-year component (a B1 field needs φ_extra in-sample, so BP needs it a fortiori), place effects held constant while places drift apart, and, for epidemic series, a level extrapolated from a window. The mixture over regimes with the training fit's φ_extra and the damped place course roughly halves the mean KS (annual .121 → .060, dengue .169 → .056) and raises the held-out log score 9 % (annual) and 70 % (dengue). BP still misses §6.2 in the worst region; OQ 6 keeps the remainder (regional drift, categories born after the fit). Adopted: ADR-0009 (`prospective.py`, `monolith.extrapolate_members`, `Expectations.prospective`). The dispersion and Laplace entries' reading that BP's miscalibration is level and untouched by dispersion holds for dispersion estimated on BP's own cells; the training fit's place-year component was what BP lacked.

## Follow-up (2026-10-05, later): the blocked origins, the outbreak lens, the unseen category

**Scenario.** `scripts/measure_bp_level.py annual|dengue ORIGIN --lean` (the earlier BP against the adopted one; artefacts `data/logs/bp_level_*_2016|2018|2020.json`, now with KS per macro-region), pooled by `scripts/bp_level_table.py --origins`; `data/dengue_outbreak_bp2.py`; `data/p5/unseen_category.py`. Fits as in `data/logs/bp_level_fits2.sh`. Regime: design-v0, same as above.

**SIM origin 2018** (test 2019–23, six fields without chapter I, plus XVIII: 7 pairs): log score −596,044 → **−522,139**; mean KS .135 → **.046**, mean worst-region .198 → **.094**; pooled KS .122 → .022; obs/exp 1.08 → 0.99. Mean KS per macro-region (N, NE, SE, S, CO), adopted: .067 .067 .052 .068 .046 (earlier BP .170 .183 .121 .100 .119): §6.2's 0.05 per region still missed in four of five, as above. Chapter I at 2018 is COVID again: KS .488 → .451, log score −635,946 → −364,852.

**Dengue origins 2016 (test 2017–21) and 2020 (2021–23).** Log score, mean KS, mean worst region:

|---|---|---|---|
| level36, block φ (earlier) | −1.43 M, .257, .339 | −1.45 M, .167, .219 | −7.55 M, .191, .271 |

The choice holds on four origins (the regimes beat level36 + φ_extra on the log score at each). Per region, 2016 and 2020 pooled: .061 .041 .069 .068 .105 (N, NE, SE, S, CO); the Centro-Oeste fails at 2020 (.165), where 2021–23 brought epidemics the fit (≤ 2020) holds as normal years. The test years of 2016 were the troughs (obs/exp 0.65): the regimes over-expect after a decline, as they under-expect after the Sul's spread.

**SIM origin 2021 (the COVID break; test 2022–23; fits ≤ 2021 hold two COVID years), reported on its own, never pooled** (`measure_bp_level.py annual 2021 --lean`, `data/logs/bp_level_annual_2021.json`): the adopted BP still beats the linear forecast with the block's φ, but nothing is calibrated. Seven fields without chapter I: log score −236,694 → **−209,614**; mean KS .166 → **.088**, mean worst-region .216 → **.126**; obs/exp 0.93 → 0.95; mean KS per macro-region (N, NE, SE, S, CO) .095 .097 .106 .081 .100. Chapter X (respiratory) is the worst (KS .314, from .375; the COVID years sit in the fit as the level to which the later years fall), IX .041, XVIII .089. Chapter I: KS .956 → .892, obs/exp 0.07 → 0.14, log score −605,067 → −165,674: the fit holds COVID-19 as normal, and 2022–23 fall far below it. Against the same seven fields at origin 2018 (KS .042, worst .092) the break roughly doubles the miss: a level learned in a pandemic is no level for the years after it. The regimes idea of the monthly grain has no annual counterpart (ADR-0009 item 2), so no rescue is adopted.

**The outbreak lens on the new BP** is in the dengue entry (dated note): the regimes predictive is calibrated but a weaker epidemic alarm (2019–23 recall 0.48 / precision 0.84 against 0.91 / 0.64 for level36 of the baseline entry; 2015–16 0.22 / 0.80 against 0.78 / 0.78). The alarm reads `history="level36"` (0.81 / 0.75, 0.49 / 0.90 with φ_extra).

**The unseen category (ADR-0011).** B34 (COVID-19) is *not* unseen in the 2019 fit of chapter I: 802 training deaths, 340 expected, 714,782 observed, so chapter I's KS .54 is the positive, not a modelling gap. Real unseen leaves: 15, four with later events (A67, B35, B96: one death each; B04 mpox: 14, eleven in 2022). Options, with B34 also treated as unseen as a test:

| option | chapter I KS / log score (B34 as unseen) | B34 events found | real unseen leaves |
|---|---|---|---|
| the fit's default (shrunk prior, 0.2–0.7 events/leaf) | .54 / −355 k | 99.1 % of events in cells p < 10⁻⁶ | mpox 0 cells |
| parent's rate (θ_cat = v_cat = 0) | .54 / −355 k | 99.6 % | 5–104 invented events/leaf; mpox 0 cells |
| zero, flagged, out of the node | **.025 / −48 k** | 0 (out of the lenses) | no cost (17 of 958,617 events) |
| zero + count + alarm at ≥ 5 events a year | .025 / −48 k | 97.9 % of events in place-years ≥ 5 | B04 2022 alarms (11); singles do not |

Adopted: the last (ADR-0011, `surprise.NEW_CATEGORY`, `extras["new_category"|"new_category_alarm"]`, checked live on chapter I at 2019: 17 events out, alarm B04 2022). The threshold rests on four examples.

**Verdict.** The adopted BP's gain holds at the blocked origins (annual mean KS .135 → .046; dengue log score at four origins −7.55 M → −2.38 M); §6.2 in the worst region stays missed (OQ 6).

## Alarm baseline and expectation (2026-10-05, ADR-0012)
