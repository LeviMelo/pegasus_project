# The level of the prospective tier BP: place drift, the training fit's φ_extra, and regimes (2026-10-05)

**Scenario.** `scripts/measure_bp_level.py annual|dengue ORIGINS` and `scripts/bp_level_table.py` (artefacts `data/logs/bp_level_<kind>_<origin>.json`, local; fits by `scripts/fit_blocks.py`). **Regime.** design-v0, contiguity fits as cached in `pegasus_home/monolith`; MAP predictive; each variant scores the same test cells (the five years after the origin) as a mixture of NBs. Annual: SIM.DO deaths, seven fields (IX, I20-I25, I60-I69, I64, I10-I15, X, XVIII) at origins 2014 (test 2015–19), 2016 (XVIII not fitted; 2017–21) and 2019 (2020–23): 20 field-origin pairs. Monthly: dengue probable cases at origins 2014 and 2018. **Counted.** The held-out log score (sum over cells, higher is better), the PIT's KS pooled over the pairs, the mean per-pair KS and worst-region KS (§6.2), obs/expected.

**Where the miss lies** (oracle decompositions, chapter IX at 2019): rescaling each year to the observed national total moves KS 0.126 → 0.101, each macro-region-year 0.094, **each place's multiplier over the four years 0.013** (log score −79.5k → −67.6k). Dengue at 2018: the national-month oracle moves the log score −3.54M → −2.31M, the place multiplier −1.26M. BP's miscentring is the place's level, and for dengue the epidemic regime; the national h is the smaller part for deaths.

- **The gain holds at every origin:** damped h + course 0.5 against the reference, log score −562,340 → −510,814 (2014, the only origin whose test years precede COVID), −488,047 → −445,475 (2016), −467,216 → −417,540 (2019); mean KS .122 → .052, .135 → .085, .106 → .048. The pooled PIT is flat (deciles .098–.111 at 2014, outer deciles .112 and .116 at 2019).
- **The h forecast is second-order:** linear, damped and flat differ by under 700 nats once the course is in (−1,378,468 / −1,373,829 / −1,374,243); the damped slope is best by log score and was adopted. A random-walk spread on the national level adds 385 nats of 1.4 M: not adopted, and the earlier forecast-variance heuristic (`laplace.forecast_variance`) is removed.
- **The place trend needs damping:** at full slope the log score is 25,000 below that at 0.5; 0.25 and 0.5 are the same.
- **Not met:** the §6.2 criterion (KS ≤ 0.03, ≤ 0.05 in every region) is missed in the worst region (mean .116); regional departures remain.
- **Chapter I (infectious) is not a calibration case:** at 2019 B34 (COVID-19) is a category the fit never saw, KS .53 whatever the variant (obs/exp 3.7); over the three origins the pooled KS stays .27 although the log score rises −1.11 M → −0.66 M.

- **Not run:** dengue origins 2016 and 2020 and SIM origins 2018 and 2021 (training ≤ 2019 against ≤ 2021, the COVID break) were queued behind the shared machine's jobs. The dengue choice rests on two origins; the break is not measured.

## Verdict

BP's predictive was miscentred because it was too narrow and too still: the block's φ without the place-year component (a B1 field needs φ_extra in-sample, so BP needs it a fortiori), place effects held constant while places drift apart, and, for epidemic series, a level extrapolated from a window. The mixture over regimes with the training fit's φ_extra and the damped place course roughly halves the mean KS (annual .121 → .060, dengue .169 → .056) and raises the held-out log score 9 % (annual) and 70 % (dengue). BP still misses §6.2 in the worst region; OQ 6 keeps the remainder (regional drift, categories born after the fit). Adopted: ADR-0009 (`prospective.py`, `monolith.extrapolate_members`, `Expectations.prospective`). The dispersion and Laplace entries' reading that BP's miscalibration is level and untouched by dispersion holds for dispersion estimated on BP's own cells; the training fit's place-year component was what BP lacked.
