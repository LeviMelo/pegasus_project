# Simulation-based calibration of the fit and its Laplace posterior

**Regime.** Pegasus_core `design-v0` (commits after 55a193c); `scripts/sbc.py`; artefact `data/probes/sbc/SIM.DO_VII_2010_2021.json`; 2026-10-06.

**What and why.** This is ARCHITECTURE §10.6.1. SIM.DO chapter VII was fitted for 2010–2021 under ADR-0024's defaults: 277 deaths, 13 geography carriers. Each of 100 replicates ran as follows:
- a truth was drawn from the fit's exact Laplace posterior (Poisson weights);
- counts were simulated from it;
- the mean was refitted at the fit's strengths;
- the rank of each tracked quantity among 99 draws of the refit's posterior was recorded.

Uniform ranks would mean the solver and the Laplace approximation are calibrated at fixed strengths. VII was chosen as the sparse case.

**Result.**
- **The effects themselves are mostly calibrated.**
  - Group levels: 10 of 13 have rank-histogram χ² p > 0.03. H40-H42 has p = 0.003; H43-H45 and H53-H54 have p ≈ 1e-20, with the truth above the draws.
  - The last year's course: p = 0.60. The first year's: p = 0.0006.
  - The age profile: p = 0.12, 0.07 and 0.0008 at three ages.
- **Sums over cells are not calibrated.**
  - Every group's expected total has the truth below 65–99 of the hundred replicates' draws (p down to 1e-183).
  - The intercept leans the same way (70 % of its ranks in the lowest decile), and the five largest places' effects the other way.

**Diagnosis.** Three explanations were tested on single replicates (an inline check in the session, kept in this note):
- **A harness defect: refuted.** The simulated total matches its truth's (1,704 against 1,673; 1,072 against 1,062), and the refit's mode reproduces the simulated total exactly.
- **The SBC design** (truths from the posterior, refit under the prior): cannot produce it. With a free intercept the exact posterior's mean total equals the observed total whatever the prior.
- **The Laplace draws: the cause.**
  - The four truths drawn from the real fit's posterior imply 1,062–2,744 deaths, against the 277 observed.
  - Within a replicate, the draws' mean total is twice the mode's.
  - Mechanism: a level with no events has a skewed exact posterior, its left tail long and its right tail cut off by the likelihood. A Gaussian at its mode gives it a symmetric right tail. The category levels' prior sd is 2.1 (τ 0.22) and the iid place sd 0.9, so exp() of that tail dominates any sum.

**Consequences.**
- The production surprise is not affected. It reads the mode's expectation (`Expectations(laplace=0)`, the default), and with draws it is centred on it (`center="plugin"`).
- The Laplace layer must not be used for count-scale functionals (totals, a field's μ with its interval) in sparse blocks until the skew is handled. Established remedies:
  - INLA's simplified Laplace (skew-corrected marginals);
  - each sparse scalar redrawn from its exact one-dimensional conditional given the rest of a draw.
- A posterior-SBC whose truths are implausible as data generators (six times the observed deaths) tests little. The SBC of a sparse block needs truths drawn under the skew correction. Chapter XIII (dense) is running.
