# The v1 solver: exact Newton on the assembled block-arrowhead Hessian (2026-10-06)

**Regime:**
- **Block:** SIM.DO chapter IX, 2010–2021, contiguity, POPSVS. U = 5,570 places, T = 12, G = 36, E = 77 leaves, K = 10 groups, 2.07 M non-empty cells, 552,046 parameters.
- **Code:** `src/pegasus_core/solver.py` (commit `4d48aa0` and its successors on design-v0).
- **Scripts:** `scratchpad/check_solver.py` (the checks below), `scripts/bench.py` (whole fits, written to `data/bench/`).
- **Machine:** calm, CPU float64 (the environment's PyTorch was CPU-only during these runs, see *Environment*).
- **Design:** ARCHITECTURE §5.3–5.5 and `docs/plans/2026-10-06-optimization.md`.

## The strengths

All runs: cold start, IX 2010–2021, `scripts/bench.py`, rows in `data/bench/2026-10-06.jsonl`, logs in `data/bench_ix_v1*.log`.

| update of the strengths | outers | result |
|---|---|---|
| v0: Fellner–Schall with each effect's Fisher diagonal for tr(ΣQ) | 12–40 | the reference fit (held-out below) |
| Fellner–Schall with **exact** traces (constrained Laplace covariance; globals exactly from S⁻¹, the rest by 32 probes solved with the factor) | stopped at 9 | linear: largest τ change 0.24 → 0.13 over outers 4–8 |
| the same, Anderson-accelerated (v0's `accelerate`) | stopped at 13 | fell to 0.077, then oscillated (0.26, 0.20): unstable with probe-estimated traces |
| "Fisher scoring" with ½τ_iτ_j tr(ΣQ_iΣQ_j) | stopped at 4 | every τ jumped by the ×10 clip each outer. **The matrix was wrong:** with no data it claims ½·rank of information where the marginal likelihood is flat |
| Newton on ρ = log τ with the observed −H of LAML (W's derivative dropped): ½δ_ij τ_j(tr_j + q_j) − ½τ_iτ_j tr(ΣQ_iΣQ_j) − τ_iτ_j xᵀQ_iΣQ_jx, spectrum floored relative to the largest eigenvalue | stopped at 9 | stable; the weakly identified (h_all, th_grp) crept ×1.25 per outer: the global floor flattened them |
| the same, floored on each component's own scale, and Fellner–Schall's step taken where it agrees in sign and goes further | **11, converged** | 282 s; φ 6.548 (v0 6.547) |

| converged configuration (32 trace probes, 16 for T, kriging directions fresh per factor) plus the LAML-gain stop | **6** | **154 s** cold; held-out 2.21828. Warm from the stored relative: 3 outers, 74 s, held-out 2.21825 |
| 8 trace probes, 16 for T, kriging directions reused over 3 factors | 29 | 497 s: cheaper outers (about 4 + 10 s), noisier gradient, many more of them |
| 8 trace probes, 4 for T | 23 | 376 s |
| 8 trace probes, no T (diagonal and R terms only) | 38 | 298 s: about 8 s per outer, but the steps lose their direction |

**Exact traces measured, probes kept.** Selected inversion (a numba Takahashi recursion on CHOLMOD's simplicial factor) plus tr(S⁻¹XᵀQX) and the kriging term agree with 256-probe Hutchinson to 3–8·10⁻⁴ for s_all, v_all, s_grp and v_grp at the stored IX fit. They took 44 s, against 16.8 s for 256 probes and about 2 s for 32. At 32 probes the trace noise moves the Newton step on log τ by about 10⁻⁴, so it is not what limits the outers. The probes are kept and the exact code was removed; a supernodal selected inversion would change the trade-off.

**A model change measured and rejected:** v_cat only on leaves with ≥ 0.1 % of the block's events and ≥ 100 events (42 of IX's 77 leaves). IX held-out deviance 2.21828 → 2.21934, time 169 → 160 s; VII (no leaf qualifies) held-out 18.21 → 19.95. The rare codes' place deviations predict, and the strengths' probes, not the leaf-place block's size, set the cost of an outer.

**Held-out 2022–2023** (787,877 deaths, `monolith.heldout`):

| fit | deviance per death | NB log-likelihood per death |
|---|---|---|
| v0 (the rank sweep's R = 0) | 2.21851 | −1.63784 |
| v1, converged | **2.21824** | **−1.63770** |

**The v1 strengths differ from v0's along the BYM ridge.** v1 has s_all 48, v_all 117, s_grp 22, v_grp at its bound; v0 had s_grp ≈ 175 with v_all shrunk. The marginal likelihood is flat along that ridge: v1 wanders on it at outers 6–8, the only outers whose change exceeds the tolerance. Convergence is therefore read from the Newton step's predicted LAML gain (½gᵀΔρ < 0.1), not from Δρ. BYM2 (O1, §4.3) is the reparametrisation that removes the ridge.

## v0 against v1 on a calm machine

IX 2010–2021, cold, `scripts/bench.py --heldout 2023`. This is the comparison the earlier v0 figures lacked: they were measured under heavy load.

| solver | seconds | outers | Newton steps | CG iterations | φ | held-out deviance per death |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| v0 | 501 | 17 | 168 | 7,768 | 6.5466 | 2.21851 |
| v1 | 154–169 | 6 | 26 | 0 | 6.5485 | 2.21828 |

**v1 is about 3× faster calm**, reaches a better optimum, and predicts at least as well. It became the default for count blocks without the interaction (`Monolith._uses_v1`); `PEGASUS_SOLVER=v0` restores v0.

Warm, from the stored relative: v1 74 s in 3 outers, against v0's 191 s in the rank sweep (which ran with other jobs). **The §5.8 budget of 20 s cold is not met.** The outer count (6) and the strengths' probes (about 10 s per outer) are what remains.

## Environment

Installing scikit-sparse 0.5 (CHOLMOD, SuiteSparse 7.10) from conda-forge did three things:
- it replaced the CUDA build of PyTorch 2.5.1 with conda-forge's CPU PyTorch 2.8;
- it downgraded pyarrow from 24 to 23;
- it removed geopandas, shapely and pillow.

A rollback to the previous revision stalled in the solver for over an hour and was stopped. The repair is targeted: force-remove PyTorch, pip-install the CUDA 12.1 build, pip-reinstall the removed packages. **Lesson:** a conda-forge package with deep dependencies goes in with `--no-deps`, and the transaction is read before it is accepted.

## Second round: the model's centring, the start, and safeguarded strengths

Same block and machine, `scripts/bench.py --heldout 2023`, logs `data/bench_sexipf.log`, `data/bench_laml.log`, `data/bench_escape.log`.

**A model defect, found while profiling the cold start.**
- `f_all` is stored as (sex × bands) and was centred within each sex, and `f_grp` the same way, so no component carried the level difference between the sexes.
- On the stored IX fit, the youngest bands were fitted at 0.57–0.77× observed in one sex and 1.4–3.0× in the other. The RW2 curves bent to make up for the missing level.
- Both are now centred over both sexes together. The sex difference is a null-space direction of the RW2 that the data inform.
- The objective at the optimum improved by about 2,270 log-likelihood units. **Held-out deviance per death went from 2.21828 to 2.21703, and NB log-likelihood from −1.63772 to −1.63716.**

**The start.**
- The marginal start (each margin against the flat rate, then centred) began at an objective of 4.3·10⁸. Zeroing θ_cat alone gives 1.6·10⁷, because the centring moved the margins' means to nowhere.
- The new start is the main-effects Poisson ML by iterative proportional fitting. It begins at 3.2·10⁶ (the optimum is 2.58·10⁶) and costs 0.06 s.

**The iid place effects are uncentred** (INLA's convention for a proper iid effect). This removes 87 of IX's 117 constraints across places: 30 kriging columns per factor instead of 117.

**The strengths:**

| change | effect |
|---|---|
| the factor the strengths read is built at the converged mean (after chord steps it was up to four steps back) | the LAML's finite-difference derivative now matches the scoring gradient (h_all −0.169 / −0.168, f_grp 45.2 / 45.7); before, h_all read 548 against −0.17 |
| the LAML evaluated at each new mean, and a step that lowered it by more than 2 units halved (mgcv) | VII converges in 5 outers (it oscillated for 40 without the safeguard, after the centring changes) |
| each gradient component shrunk by twice its probe standard error | IX's place strengths stop wandering on Hutchinson noise (±13 on v_all's gradient) |
| global pairs of tr(ΣQ_iΣQ_j) exact from the globals' covariance block, place traces from the same probes as T | the strengths' update 8.5 → about 6 s |
| the many-column Schur products on the CPU instead of CuPy (float64: 0.08 s against 0.15–0.26 s) | about 1 s per factor |
| clip ×10 → ×100 | the first two outers no longer spent at the clip |
| **rejected:** a strength whose step overran the clip sent to its boundary at once | from τ = 1, s_all and v_all went to 10⁶ and stayed (the LAML is flat out there); held-out −1.63793 |

**The gap the remaining W term leaves.** The LAML's derivative and the scoring gradient (which drops W's dependence on the mean) differ for s_all (−730 / −610) and v_cat (881 / 1,969). The two fixed points are about 4 % and 1 % of τ apart.

**VII** (a small block) converges in 5 outers, 92 s, φ 5.3. Its held-out NB log-likelihood is −10.27 per death, against −10.10 in a 17-outer v1 run under the old centring (φ 0.54). The place strengths are weak (s_all 6.5, v_grp 0.7). The mean is fitted by Poisson quasi-likelihood without a scale, so in a sparse block the place effects can absorb the overdispersion (OPEN_QUESTIONS 8).
